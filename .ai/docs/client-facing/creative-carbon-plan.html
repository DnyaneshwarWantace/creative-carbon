Four rules every screen follows

1It looks like the register. Same columns, same order, same words: Chindi, Kushan, Daylight, Die No., B-stage, Batch, O.B. Tab order follows the paper. Enter saves the row and moves down.
2Only what they write today is required. Printed-but-blank fields (press top/bottom temp, curing time, heating slip) exist but are switched off. The owner switches them on in Masters → Register settings.
3One data-entry person keys everything for 4–5 months. Every register has an Excel template and upload. Client confirmed on the day they will hire a graduate for this.
4Phone first, weak signal. Installable app, entry screens keep typing when the hotspot drops, three-decimal kg everywhere, dates typed as dd/mm/yy. No tablets on the floor at first (confirmed again in the audio).

Production is not driven by orders. They buy in container loads and press to stock about a month ahead. The order only allocates finished lots. The link from orders back to production is the shortfall list on the owner's plan (Stage 8) and the "Allocated to" column on FG inspection (Stage 7). Moulded dies are the exception: dies belong to a customer and the moulding register carries the order quantity.

The page pattern every register uses

FROM DERMAT

Dermat's order and stage pages already have this shape (header, stage strip, work area, "before this stage" panel, history). Every Creative Carbon register gets the same four pages so staff learn one layout.

1. Register view (list)

Looks like the open book: one day or one month at a time, columns exactly as printed, totals in a sticky footer. Filters: date, department equipment (dryer / press / machine), status. Buttons: New entry, Upload Excel, Print register.

2. Entry page

The paper form on screen. Draft saves itself every few seconds and survives a dropped connection. "Post" checks the rules, moves stock and locks the record. Warnings (tolerance, RC/VC, trim) show inline in amber and never stop posting.

3. Record detail page

Header strip with number, date, equipment, status and sign-offs. Main area: the posted form, read-only. Right column: Came from (lots consumed, with links), Went to (lots produced and where they went next), Warnings logged, History (who changed what). Reopen within 24 h with a reason, later only with the manager right (same as Dermat).

4. Lot page

One page per lot number: item, quantity left, store, age / expiry, parents and children as a tree, every movement, QC results, lab reports, which order it is allocated to. This is the page that answers "where is it kept" and "what went into it".

What the photos and the recording changed

CHECKED

Each register photo was read field by field against the brief. These change how we build.

✓Batch report "2/24" is "sheets of this thickness / total sheets in the batch". F/01 = 10 + 4 + 4 = 18, F/02 = 10 + 8 = 18, F/03 = 10 + 6 + 6 + 2 = 24, F/04 = 10 + 1 + 6 + 1 = 18. The total is calculated.IMG_7787
✓Batch total weight is the sum of the loading weights. F/03: 25 mm 235.8 + 15 mm 418.7 + 10 mm 277.8 + ten 1.5 mm sheets ≈ 64.0 gives 996.300. So the total is calculated from the loading register, not typed twice.IMG_7785 · 7787
✓A daylight holds one or more sheets. Press 2: daylights 4–9 each hold a 15 mm and a 10 mm sheet; daylight 3 holds ten 1.5 mm sheets (6.200 / 6.400). The model is batch → daylight → sheets.IMG_7785
✓The dryer report is two tables side by side. Left: cloth rows (cloth, GSM, Kushan, weights, coated nos, resin, RC, VC). Right: a two-hourly slot log (8, 10, 12, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10) with DBP, oleic acid and Remarks. Remarks is the output in kg per slot: 637.000 + 286.400 + 531.100 + 116.160 = 1570.66, the ringed day total. Raw cloth (861) and coated nos (786) are also ringed day totals.IMG_7783
✓The moulding register's "Total" row is the running total against the order. Machine 10: order 10,000, this shift 36, total 8,107. Machine 12: order 100, shift 17, total 97. The system adds up all shifts for that die and order.IMG_7786
✓"10 ± 10" on the thickness register is the weave 10×10. Readings below it are 25.5–25.8 mm. Target thickness comes from the batch line; the tolerance needs a number from the client.IMG_7777
✓Despatch weighment is one weight per sheet. "10-16" = 16 sheets of 10 mm with exactly 16 weights, five per line, row totals to 3 decimals. The packing screen takes per-sheet weights.IMG_7776
!FG inspection "Quantity KGS" holds piece counts (1, 2, 6, 20, 18 sheets of 8×4). We record pieces and work out kg from lot weights.IMG_7778

!Methanol is used every day (44–600 kg) but blank on the resin batch form. So some chemicals go somewhere other than resin batches, probably coating. The chemical register cannot be fully derived from resin batches: we add a chemical issue entry. Confirm where methanol, DBP and oleic acid go.IMG_7759 · 7758

Small things that are easy to miss

CHECKLIST
•Every weight is kg to three decimals; every percentage two; thickness two; never floats.
•Ditto marks and "NIL" on paper must work in entry and upload.
•Gap-free batch numbers that staff can check against the book; a deleted draft does not use up a number.
•Old-style numbers (F/61/9/26) are accepted on import and searchable.
•Sign-off fields (chemist, in-charge, three on moulding) record who and when but never stop posting.
•B-stage sold to a customer needs its own invoice format (sample promised) and is priced per kg.
•Expired B-stage stays visible and saleable with an override reason; only pressing is blocked after 10 days.
•Trim scrap never returns to stock.
•Bought-in finished goods and raw material sold directly are normal documents, not workarounds.
•The die booking check covers both shifts and both registers, and the day-ahead production plan.
•Tubes and moulded parts differ by machine: the item form is decided by the press used.
•Accounts and sales sit in Ahmedabad, the plant in Kanera: two locations, one system, India time everywhere.
•The owner travels 10–12 days a month, so his overview, the B-stage board and stock must work one-handed on a phone.
•Printed reports (chemical register, daily batch report, FG inspection, weighment) carry the form code and rev. no. as on the paper.
•Reopening a posted record reverses its stock moves; after 24 hours only a manager can reopen.
•Users' Hindi labels never translate plant words (Chindi, Kushan, Daylight, Die No., B-stage).
