What we found in the code today

SURVEY · 9 OCT

The pages exist and link well. What is thin is the story behind each record and the way to fix a mistake.

1
History says what happened, never what changed. CRM, purchase, plant and vendor bills keep a list of "action · by · when · note". The order has an event log. None of them keep the old and new value of a field, so "who changed the rate from 312 to 298?" cannot be answered.
2
Customers, vendors, items, masters and access have no history at all. Only "last updated" is kept. A changed GSTIN, payment term or user right leaves no trace.Access changes are a security gap: nobody can see who gave a right.
3
Several records cannot be corrected. Thickness readings, lab reports (edits overwrite an issued report), bought-in goods, damage write-offs, GRN quantities and stocktake have no correction path. Invoices have no credit note.
4
12 record types have no detail page: follow-up, salesperson activity, price list rate, despatch, credit / debit note, tally push, plan for the day, stock movement, stocktake / opening stock, store adjustment, offline sync clash, settings lists.
5
Four history shapes, shown four ways. Each module writes and draws its own history. Some reopens need a reason, some do not. When a record is corrected, nobody is told.
✓
Good base to build on. 22 record types already have reopen, cancel, fail, void or reverse in code (resin, coating, press, moulding, cutting, FG, chemical issue, PO, indent, GRN return, proforma, invoice, payment, vendor bill, order cancel, stage reopen with time limits and a manager right, despatch reverse). Stock always moves by entries, so counter-entries are natural.

Correcting a mistake: one rule for the whole system

NOTHING DELETED

Every record shows a Correct this… button that opens one dialog. The same rules apply everywhere.

1 · Pick what to do

Only the actions this person may take on this record, at this moment, are offered: edit, reopen, revise, reverse, cancel, void, credit note.

2 · See what it undoes

A preview lists every stock movement that will be reversed, every stage that will reopen, and every person who will be told.

3 · See what blocks it

If a later record already used this one (a resin lot already coated, a lot already despatched), the dialog names it with a link and says what to correct first.

4 · Give the reason

Required. Short list of common reasons plus free text. Shown in the timeline forever.

5 · Counter-entries, not deletes

Stock is put back by a reversing movement linked to the original. Money is corrected by revision, credit / debit note or void. Both stay visible.

6 · Safe with two people

The record version is checked when saving: if someone else changed it in between, the correction stops and asks to reload.
